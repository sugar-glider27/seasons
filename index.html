The question to carry forward

When does a warmer world feel most different?

Seasonal change can be obscured by an annual average. Comparing monthly warming across regions makes it easier to see which parts of the calendar shift most, and how much the answer depends on the emissions pathway.

Data: CMIP6 monthly near-surface air temperature (tas), averaged over land within broad starter regions. The preview fallback is illustrative; model summaries are exploratory, not local forecasts.
